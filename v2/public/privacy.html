Privacy Policy & Messaging Terms

Jensen and Juhl LLC · Last updated 10 August 2026

Jensen and Juhl LLC (“Jensen and Juhl”, “we”) operates jensenandjuhl.com and the brands Drapery Society and XCTN Production. This page explains what we collect and how we use text messaging with the crew who work with us.

Text messages to crew

We send text messages only to people who work with us as crew or contractors, and only about their work.

  • How you opt in. You provide your mobile number when you set up your crew profile. At that point we tell you we may text you about your gigs. Giving us your number is how you opt in.
  • What we send. Job logistics only: call times, address or schedule changes, and messages about work you have been offered or accepted. We do not send marketing or promotional texts.
  • How often. Only when something about your work needs your attention. There is no recurring or scheduled campaign.
  • How to stop. Reply STOP to any message and we will not text you again. Reply HELP for help, or email info@jensenandjuhl.com.
  • Cost. Message and data rates may apply. We do not charge you for messages.
  • Carriers. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opting out of text messages does not affect your work with us. We will reach you by email instead.

What we collect

  • Crew: name, phone number, email, address, emergency contact, a profile photo, and the tax and identity documents required to pay you lawfully. If you use our app while checked in to a job, we record your location at that moment so we can confirm attendance on site.
  • Website visitors: anything you choose to send us through the contact form.

How we use it

  • To schedule you, pay you, and meet our tax and contracting obligations.
  • To contact you about work.
  • Nothing else. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

We share information only with service providers who help us operate — payments, messaging, and hosting — and only what they need to do that job, or where the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep crew records for as long as we work together and afterwards for as long as tax and employment law requires. You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete anything we are not legally required to keep. Email info@jensenandjuhl.com and we will respond.
